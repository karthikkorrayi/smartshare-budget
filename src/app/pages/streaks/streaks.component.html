How streaks work

  • Logging — any entry you add that day, or “Nothing spent today”.
  • No-spend — a day with no spending. Card bill payments, top-ups and money you move don't count as spending.
  • Within limit — spending at or under your daily limit.
  • A day counts once it's over; today joins as soon as it qualifies. Days before you joined never count.
